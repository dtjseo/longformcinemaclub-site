Cinema for the long stretches.

Reel 03 — Why Longform

Most film content is built to grab your attention. Ours is built to hold it — gently, for hours. Every compilation gathers decades of a filmmaker's interviews into one continuous listen, so Kubrick can talk you through a long work session, or Scorsese can ride shotgun on a cross-state drive.

No jump cuts. No sudden volume spikes. Just the longest conversations about cinema on the internet.
