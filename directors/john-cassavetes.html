The Story

John Cassavetes, In His Own Words

This compilation gathers decades of John Cassavetes's interviews into one continuous listen — arranged to flow gently from the early years to the late reflections, easy to put on in the background while you work, study, or drive.

What's Inside This Compilation

John Cassavetes started as a working actor in Hollywood before turning that money into something almost nobody else was doing in the 1950s and '60s: independent film, financed and made on his own terms. Shadows, Faces, Husbands, A Woman Under the Influence, and Love Streams grew out of long rehearsal, real improvisation, and a refusal to smooth performances into something tidy.

These interviews find him talking the way he directed — unguarded, argumentative, deeply serious about actors and emotional truth even when the tone is loose. He talks about financing films himself by taking studio acting jobs, about his creative partnership with Gena Rowlands, and about a version of filmmaking built entirely around people rather than plot.

Why Listen for Hours?

Because that's when it stops being an interview and becomes company. Longform Cinema Club builds every compilation for the long stretches of life — the overnight drive, the deep work session, the long run, the quiet hour before bed. Nothing jolts you. Press play and let the conversation carry you.
