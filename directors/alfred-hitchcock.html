The Story

Alfred Hitchcock, In His Own Words

This compilation gathers decades of Alfred Hitchcock's interviews into one continuous listen — arranged to flow gently from the early years to the late reflections, easy to put on in the background while you work, study, or drive.

What's Inside This Compilation

Alfred Hitchcock built the modern thriller almost single-handedly, from his start in the British silent film industry through the string of American classics that made him a household name: Rebecca, Notorious, Rear Window, Vertigo, North by Northwest, Psycho, and The Birds. This compilation gathers his interviews across that career, from a young director explaining silent-film technique to the elder statesman reflecting on fifty years of Hollywood.

Hitchcock's interview voice is as deliberate as his filmmaking — a dry, unhurried English cadence that turns technical explanations of suspense, editing, and audience manipulation into something closer to storytelling. He explains the MacGuffin, the difference between shock and suspense, and the mechanics behind Psycho's shower scene with the same composed wit he brought to his famous on-screen cameos.

Why Listen for Hours?

Because that's when it stops being an interview and becomes company. Longform Cinema Club builds every compilation for the long stretches of life — the overnight drive, the deep work session, the long run, the quiet hour before bed. Nothing jolts you. Press play and let the conversation carry you.
